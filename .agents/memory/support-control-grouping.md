---
name: Support control grouping
description: Why Northstar support actions share a frame while remaining separate destinations.
---

Keep Help & Support and Support Centre as separate, familiar actions inside one restrained shared frame. Notifications should remain outside that group.

**Why:** The user confirmed that the shared frame makes the actions feel collectively related, like the dashboard’s AI alert and Business Insights treatment, without collapsing their different purposes or crowding the utility area.

**How to apply:** Preserve the outer-frame relationship and internal divider when evolving the header. New support-related actions should be placed within the appropriate existing destination rather than added as another loose utility control.
